# The Git History of Us

> "A version-controlled timeline of moments, milestones, and everything in between."

This repository is a digital journal dedicated to my relationship with Srija. Instead of a traditional diary, this is a living, breathing log of our journey—tracked, committed, and saved. Whether it is a remarkable incident, a simple conversation, or just a day where Shree made me smile, it gets documented here.

##  Initialization

* **`init` (The Beginning):** September 15, 2026
* **Primary Collaborators:** Proyash & Srija

##  Repository Structure

The journal is organized chronologically to keep the timeline clean and easy to navigate:

* **`YYYY/`** → Grouped by year.
* **`MM_Month/`** → Grouped by month.
* **`DD_Title.md`** → Daily entries, memorable dates, and specific incidents.




* **`assets/`** → Photos, screenshots, and embedded memories.

##  Tags & Terminology

To keep the logs organized, entries might contain specific highlights:

* **Milestones:** Monthly anniversaries, firsts, and major dates.
* **The Little Things:** Random moments, being called "babay" or "shona," and everyday happiness.
* **Quotes:** Memorable things she said that belong in the permanent record.
